Worried it will hurt — or that you'll be talked into something?

Both worries are normal, and both are answerable. Implant placement is done under local anaesthetic, by a surgeon who performs this work at hospital consultant level — most patients tell us it was far easier than they expected. And your consultation is a conversation, not a pitch: you'll leave with a written plan and printed estimate to consider at home, in your own time. We spend real time with our patients, we use good-quality materials, and we'd rather you make the right decision than a fast one.

Questions we're asked most

Placement is done under local anaesthetic, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure — most patients describe it as easier than a tooth extraction. Some soreness for a few days afterwards is normal and usually settles with ordinary painkillers. Having a hospital consultant surgeon place the implant also means the surgery itself is as precise and gentle as it can be.

Single implants start from £2,950. If your X-ray shows you need preparatory work, a bone graft is £750 and a sinus lift £1,450 — and we'll only recommend either when it's genuinely required. Implant-secured dentures range from £3,000 to £5,000. Whatever your plan involves, you'll have every figure in a written, itemised estimate before you commit to anything.

Often, yes. Bone grafting and sinus lifts can rebuild support where bone has receded — exactly the kind of surgery our in-house consultant surgeon handles routinely. Yes — implant-secured dentures clip onto implants placed in the jaw, so they stay firmly in place while you eat, talk, and laugh. No adhesive, no clicking, no bracing yourself before biting into anything. They're planned around your clinical needs and comfort, and cost between £3,000 and £5,000.
