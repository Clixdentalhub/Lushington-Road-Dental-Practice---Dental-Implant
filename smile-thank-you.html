Thank you — your place is reserved

Your Smile Makeover Experience Day registration has been received. The date is being confirmed now — and as a registered patient, you'll be offered a place before anyone else. Our reception team will call you within one working day.

What happens next

  1. We call you

    A member of our reception team calls within one working day to confirm your registration and answer any questions. If we can't reach you, we'll try again and email too.

  2. You hear the date first

    As soon as the Experience Day date is confirmed, registered patients are offered places first — spaces are limited, so you're at the front of the queue.

  3. Your Experience Day

    Your complimentary 3D smile scan to preview your future smile, your written aligner plan and quote, and over £1,000 of complimentary extras — whitening and retainers included.

Prefer to speak to us sooner?

Call the practice on 07576 546311 and mention your Smile Makeover Experience Day registration. We're open Mon/Wed/Thu 8–6, Tue 8–7, Fri 8–4, and Sat 9–1.

Worth thinking about before your day

Nothing to prepare — but a moment on these makes your Experience Day even more useful.

  • What you'd most love to change about your smile — crooked, crowded, stained, chipped, or all of it.
  • Any photos of smiles you love — they help us understand exactly the result you're picturing.
  • Whether you've had braces before and when — it shapes how we plan your aligners.
  • The questions you most want answered — write them down and bring them along.
